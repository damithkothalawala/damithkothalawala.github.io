# Akuru public distribution

By Damith Rushika Kothalawala.

Website: https://damithkothalawala.github.io/akuru/

The app, encrypted font catalog and update manifest are public. Application source and catalog key management are private. Keys are embedded in the app to allow decryption; catalog encryption does not provide secrecy from someone inspecting the app.

Font previews are rendered in advance and bundled in the app. Source-linked font binaries remain at their original upstream hosts and retain their authors' usage terms.

Wijesekara keyboard: Dasun Sameera Weerasinghe, https://github.com/sdasun/mac-wijesekara, MIT license.

Wikipedia specimen and its rendered article previews: Sinhala Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Universal macOS 13+ app; ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
